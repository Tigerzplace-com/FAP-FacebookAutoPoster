## ⚡ Get started in 5 minutes

1. **Install.** Download FAP from [tools.tigerzplace.com](https://tools.tigerzplace.com/facebook-auto-poster/). In Chrome open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked** and choose the folder that contains `manifest.json`.
2. **Log in to Facebook** in the same Chrome profile.
3. **Open FAP** by clicking its icon. Go to **Groups** and load your joined groups.
4. **Build a campaign** in **Campaigns**: write your post, choose your groups, set the delay.
5. **Review and Launch.** Run the checks, send a test post to one group if you like, then press **Launch**.

When it finishes, you get a summary with every post link. The full history lives in **Activity**.

> 👉 **Tip:** start with 5 to 10 groups to see how it works, then scale up.

## ✨ What you can do

### Post faster
- **Post to many groups at once.** The first post goes out immediately, with waits only between posts.
- **Add photos.** Drag and drop JPEG, PNG or WebP, with a live upload bar and preview.
- **Write variations.** FAP rotates your versions across groups so posts are not identical.
- **Add link previews.** Pick which link in your post becomes the preview card.
- **Schedule posts.** Choose a future date and time and Facebook publishes it for you.
- **Reuse your work.** Save campaigns and group lists, then run, duplicate or edit them in one click.

### Stay safe
- **Smart delay.** FAP recommends a wait time for your group count and warns you before a risky setting.
- **Safety Break.** Pause automatically every N posts and resume on its own.
- **Smart failure handling.** Groups you cannot post to are skipped, five failures in a row auto-pause the run, and serious Facebook warnings stop it. Expired sessions are refreshed for you.
- **Pending-approval skip.** Groups that already hold a post of yours for admin approval are skipped.
- **Retry once.** Temporary errors get one automatic retry after 8 seconds.

### See what is happening
- **Run control in the top bar.** Progress, time to the next post, Pause, Resume and Stop, on every page.
- **Dashboard.** Posts today, pending posts, 7-day success rate and lifetime total, with a live log.
- **Activity log.** Filter by Posted, Pending, Failed, Skipped or Breaks, open any post, export to CSV.
- **Groups page.** Pictures, member counts, last visited time and a details panel for each group.

### Keep your data
- **Backup and transfer.** Export campaigns, group lists and preferences, and import them on another computer. You see a preview first, and your Facebook login and license are never included. Free on every plan.
- **Light and dark themes.** Follow your system or choose one in Settings.

## 🛡️ Posting safely

- Use the recommended delay and prefer a **range** over a fixed time. It looks more natural.
- Rotate **variations** instead of repeating one post.
- Turn on **Safety Break** for big runs and take breaks between campaigns.
- Watch for repeated failures. They are an early sign of rate limiting.

Want the details? Read [Is Facebook auto posting safe?](https://tigerzplace.com/is-facebook-auto-posting-safe/) and [Facebook posting limits 2026](https://tigerzplace.com/facebook-posting-limits-2026/).
