## Facebook Auto Poster v2.3

**The New Look**

---

### What's New

**A new layout** — A sidebar with Dashboard, Campaigns, Groups, Activity, Insights and
Settings. The top bar shows the run, your license and the account you are posting as on
every page.

**Light and dark themes** — Choose System, Light or Dark in Settings.

**Campaigns in four steps** — Write, Audience, Timing and safety, and Review and launch,
with a live preview of the post on the right. Your saved campaigns live on the new
Campaigns page with Run, Open, Duplicate, Export and Delete.

**Review before you launch** — One list checks your post, groups, delay, safety break,
photos and license. See when every post will go out, and send a test post to one group
first.

**Run control on every page** — Progress, the time to the next post, Pause, Resume and
Stop in the top bar. Stop asks you to confirm first.

**New Dashboard and Activity log** — Posts today, posts pending approval, seven-day
success rate and lifetime posts, plus a live run monitor. Filter the log, open any post,
and export it as CSV.

**Groups page** — Pictures, member counts and last visited time. Click a group for its
details, and keep your saved lists on their own tab.

**Backup and transfer** — Export your campaigns, group lists and preferences, and import
them on another computer or browser, with a preview before anything changes. Your
Facebook login and license are never included. Free on every plan.

**Clearer license messages** — Plain words when your license is ending, has ended or
needs an update, and what you can still do in the meantime.

**A new logo** — In the toolbar, on the extension page and as the default profile
picture. The Tigerzplace logo follows your theme.

**Anonymous usage stats (optional)** — Counts only, such as runs and screens opened.
Never your posts, groups or Facebook account. Turn it off in Settings > Preferences >
Privacy.

### What's Improved

**Settings in one place** — License and plan, Backup and transfer, Preferences,
Notifications, and Updates and help.

**Easier to read** — Larger small text, clearer keyboard focus, and tables that scroll
inside their card.

**Updates and help** — Video tutorials opens the YouTube playlist and What's new opens
the release notes on GitHub.

### What's Fixed

**Latest version stays visible** — Shown even when your license is read from the saved
copy.

**No false update prompts** — Download and What's new are hidden when the version found
is older than the one you have installed.

**Plan details on the sidebar card** — Separators and dashes show correctly.

**Loading on some systems** — FAP now loads where capital and small letters in file
names are treated as different.

---

### Upgrade Notes

No migration needed. Replace all files in your existing FAP folder with the new version
(don't mix old and new files), then click Reload on `chrome://extensions`. All saved
campaigns, group lists, and settings are fully preserved.
